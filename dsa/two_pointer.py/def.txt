Two Pointers

Scenario:
Look for problems where you need to iterate through the array using two pointers at the same time.
The pointers usually start from two different positions (beginning–end or slow–fast).
Used when comparing, pairing, or shrinking a range in the array.

Clue:
Problem mentions a sorted array, pair with target, compare both ends, remove duplicates, or find pairs/triplets.
Keywords: sorted, two numbers, pair sum, left and right, fast and slow.
